How a file moves through the counter

Every request — a stamp, a policy, a server — is handled the same way. The steps are written down before we start, so you always know what is outstanding and what happens next.

  1. 01

    We check the live requirement

    Every scheme changes its documents, its deadline and its form. We read the current portal requirement before quoting anything, so the price covers the work as it stands today.

  2. 02

    We fix the document list

    You get a written list of what to bring, in what form, and how many copies. Most visits come back twice because one paper was missing or wrongly issued.

  3. 03

    We file it and hand you the receipt

    The application goes in with an acknowledgement you can quote. For physical submission you get a tracking reference; for online work, the submission ID and the date it landed.

  4. 04

    We follow it to closure

    Filing is not the job. We track the application to approval, release or dispatch, and tell you when the outcome is on record.

One working day to reply

An inquiry reaches the service owner, not a queue. You get a reply with the document list, the fee and the expected date.

Fees quoted before the work

Government fee, our service charge and any third-party cost are itemised separately. Nothing is added at the end of the job.

Documents handled in-house

Identity papers stay at the counter. Nothing is photographed for marketing or shared with third parties beyond the filing itself.

Questions people ask at the counter

What documents should I bring?
Each service has its own list, and it changes when the portal changes. Send the service through the inquiry form and you will get a written list of what to bring, in what form and how many copies — before you travel to the counter.
Do you charge a service fee on top of the government fee?
Yes, and we show it separately. Every quote itemises the government fee, our service charge and any third-party cost, so you can see the whole figure before agreeing to the work.
Can you file an application from home without me visiting?
For most portal-based services, yes. We need scanned copies of your documents and a signed authorisation, and the application is filed in your name with the acknowledgement sent to you.
How long does an application take?
That is the authority's timeline, not ours. What we control is the filing date and the follow-up. We tell you the realistic window when we quote, and we tell you the moment the status changes.
Do you handle AMC and ongoing support for the systems you build?
Yes. Every engagement carries a defined response time, preventive visit schedule and a spares commitment for contracted machines. Those terms are written into the agreement rather than agreed case by case.
Which areas do you serve?
The counter in Shivajinagar serves walk-in clients from across Pune. Engineering and support work is delivered on site across the metropolitan area, and remotely elsewhere in India.
